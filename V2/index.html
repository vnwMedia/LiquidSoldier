A portion of the proceeds go to Lt. Dennis W. Zilinski II Memorial Fund

A portion of qualifying proceeds from Liquid Soldier supports the mission of the Lt. Dennis W. ZILINSKI II Memorial Fund, in honoring and supporting our nation’s heroes. The foundation’s primary focus is to raise funds that are used to train service dogs which are then placed with veterans who suffer from PTSD and TBI (Traumatic Brain Injuries).
